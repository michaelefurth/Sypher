The Solutions Index

Every puzzle has a solution.

The problems that come up most in first conversations, in roughly the words people use, each with what Sypher does about it. Search in your own words or filter by practice.

Showing 48 of 48 problems we solve most often

  1. Launch

    From idea to operating company.

  2. 01“I’ve run the numbers on a napkin. I need someone to run them properly.”Launch

    The solutionA monthly model with a base case and a stress case, carried to break-even volume and ending in a go / no-go call in plain words. Sometimes the call is no.

    The Launch practice
  3. 02“The bank wants a business plan by the end of the month.”Launch

    The solutionPro forma statements and a use-of-funds table drawn from the model, with a narrative that answers underwriting questions before they come up.

    The Launch practice
  4. 03“We signed the lease. Now what do we register first?”Launch

    The solutionA setup sequence that puts registrations and banking in the right order, so nothing stalls waiting on a document you could have filed in week one.

    The Launch practice
  5. 04“Next to the big firms, we look like a side project.”Launch

    The solutionPositioning and a capability statement that say plainly what you do and for whom, without overstating your size. Buyers notice when you do.

    The Launch practice
  6. 05“Everyone could be a customer. That’s the problem.”Launch

    The solutionOne customer segment to win first and an offer priced to the contribution margin you need, laid out as a 90-day plan with weekly actions.

    The Launch practice
  7. 06“Investors keep asking about our unit economics, and we keep guessing.”Launch

    The solutionA model that shows contribution margin per unit and how it moves with volume. The deck and data room checklist are then built to match it.

    The Launch practice

  8. Grow

    Be found. Be chosen.

  9. 07“Some months the phone won’t stop ringing. Then it goes quiet for weeks.”Grow

    The solutionSegmented target lists worked on a fixed follow-up cadence, with stage conversion reviewed on a weekly scorecard so the quiet months show up early.

    The Grow practice
  10. 08“Search for our trade in our own town and we’re on page three.”Grow

    The solutionA complete Google Business Profile and consistent NAP across directories, then schema markup and pages written to how buyers actually search.

    The Grow practice
  11. 09“We post on social media because we feel like we should.”Grow

    The solutionPositioning and an ideal customer profile first, then a channel plan sized to the budget, with one number per channel that says whether to keep going.

    The Grow practice
  12. 10“Our best clients came from referrals, and I couldn’t tell you how.”Grow

    The solutionA referral-partner program with collateral each type of partner can hand to a client, and a CRM field that records who referred whom.

    The Grow practice
  13. 11“Half the deals in our CRM died months ago.”Grow

    The solutionPipeline stages with written exit criteria and required fields, plus a weekly CRM hygiene routine short enough that people actually do it.

    The Grow practice
  14. 12“Our work is good. Our brochure isn’t.”Grow

    The solutionCollateral written and designed to one standard, starting with the piece your salespeople hand over most often.

    The Grow practice

  15. Run

    Operations that don’t depend on you being in every room.

  16. 13“If I take a week off, things stop.”Run

    The solutionSOPs for the work that repeats and decision rights written down, so most questions get answered without reaching you.

    The Run practice
  17. 14“Every project runs late, and we find out at the end.”Run

    The solutionA critical path and risk register for each project, with change orders priced and signed before the extra work starts.

    The Run practice
  18. 15“I spend Sunday nights on paperwork.”Run

    The solutionA back-office review of vendor terms and compliance deadlines, with renewals on one calendar and the repetitive steps automated where it pays.

    The Run practice
  19. 16“We find out we had a bad month weeks after it ended.”Run

    The solutionA weekly scorecard of a few leading numbers, each with an owner, pulled from systems you already use.

    The Run practice
  20. 17“New hires shadow someone for months and still ask the same questions.”Run

    The solutionAn onboarding plan built on written SOPs, with a role scorecard that says what good looks like at 30 and 90 days.

    The Run practice
  21. 18“Our leadership meeting is a status update nobody needs.”Run

    The solutionAn operating rhythm with a fixed agenda: the scorecard first, open issues second, and decisions logged with an owner and a date.

    The Run practice

  22. Modernize

    Technology that earns its keep.

  23. 19“Everyone says we should be using AI. I don’t know what for.”Modernize

    The solutionA workflow audit to find the repetitive intake and drafting work, then one scoped pilot with human review, measured in hours returned per week.

    The Modernize practice
  24. 20“I tell people not to look at our website.”Modernize

    The solutionA fast, accessible site that passes Core Web Vitals and that your own staff can edit without calling a developer.

    The Modernize practice
  25. 21“We type the same customer details into four different systems.”Modernize

    The solutionForms wired to the CRM and the accounting system, so a record is entered once and the re-keying stops.

    The Modernize practice
  26. 22“We pay for software I’ve never seen anyone open.”Modernize

    The solutionEvery subscription listed with its cost and its owner. What nobody owns gets cancelled, and what stays gets set up properly.

    The Modernize practice
  27. 23“A former employee might still have the passwords.”Modernize

    The solutionMFA on every account and access set by role in Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, with an offboarding checklist that removes leavers the same day.

    The Modernize practice
  28. 24“Month-end reporting eats two days.”Modernize

    The solutionData cleaned once at the source and a dashboard built on top of it, so the monthly report is a refresh instead of a project.

    The Modernize practice

  29. Fund

    Capital and programs, pursued with rigor.

  30. 25“There’s grant money out there. We can’t tell which of it is for us.”Fund

    The solutionAn eligibility screen against each NOFO, then a ranked shortlist showing the deadline and cost share for every program worth pursuing.

    The Fund practice
  31. 26“We applied last year and got a form rejection.”Fund

    The solutionA read of the reviewer comments, then a rewrite built on a logic model, with a budget that ties to every activity in the narrative.

    The Fund practice
  32. 27“We need two more people and can’t carry them through training.”Fund

    The solutionOn-the-Job Training and Work-Based Learning programs mapped to your hiring plan with the regional workforce board. OJT reimburses part of a new hire’s wages while they train.

    The Fund practice
  33. 28“The application asks for a UEI. What’s a UEI?”Fund

    The solutionSAM.gov registration and a UEI, started first. Registration can take weeks, and federal applications can’t be submitted without it.

    The Fund practice
  34. 29“The RFP is eighty pages and due in three weeks.”Fund

    The solutionA compliance matrix that maps every requirement to a page of the response, then the capability statement and proposal written against it.

    The Fund practice
  35. 30“We won the grant. Now the reporting starts.”Fund

    The solutionA post-award reporting calendar, with expenses recorded on the award’s reimbursement basis so draws go through and the next application starts clean.

    The Fund practice

  36. Lead

    Senior leadership, at the scale you need.

  37. 31“We need a COO. We can’t afford a COO.”Lead

    The solutionA fractional operator on a monthly retainer, acting as owner of record for agreed objectives on the days each month when they matter.

    The Lead practice
  38. 32“This initiative has been ‘next quarter’ for a year.”Lead

    The solutionAn owner of record for the initiative, working to a weekly cadence with KPIs that show whether it moved.

    The Lead practice
  39. 33“Our operations head left last month.”Lead

    The solutionInterim leadership that keeps the operating cadence running, then a written handover the permanent hire can use from the first day.

    The Lead practice
  40. 34“I make the big calls alone and second-guess every one.”Lead

    The solutionA standing advisory session, prepared from your numbers, where the decision gets argued out before you make it.

    The Lead practice
  41. 35“We hired a senior leader and it didn’t work out.”Lead

    The solutionRun the function fractionally first, then write the role from what it actually needed and hire into a job that has already been done once.

    The Lead practice
  42. 36“Sales promises things operations hears about on Monday.”Lead

    The solutionA defined handoff from sales to operations, with required fields and a short weekly meeting, so commitments are seen before they come due.

    The Lead practice

  43. Invent

    Biomedical & formulation R&D, from first principles.

  44. 37“I’m a clinician with a device idea and no idea what comes next.”Invent

    The solutionWritten user needs and design inputs first, then a prior art search and a staged roadmap with a decision gate before each round of spending.

    The Invent practice
  45. 38“The formulation works on paper. We haven’t made a batch yet.”Invent

    The solutionExcipient review and a design of experiments, then stability testing scoped before you commit to a contract lab or CDMO.

    The Invent practice
  46. 39“Is this a 510(k) or a De Novo? Nobody will give us a straight answer.”Invent

    The solutionDevice classification and predicate device research, organized as a brief for your regulatory counsel, who makes the call and the submission.

    The Invent practice
  47. 40“We need a prototype and don’t know who builds these.”Invent

    The solutionContract manufacturers and material suppliers shortlisted against a written specification, with their quotes compared side by side.

    The Invent practice
  48. 41“We’re pre-revenue and the lab bills are real.”Invent

    The solutionAn SBIR Phase I and II strategy timed to your development milestones, so non-dilutive money pays for the riskiest work.

    The Invent practice
  49. 42“Our R&D history is in two notebooks and somebody’s inbox.”Invent

    The solutionDesign controls habits from day one, so the design history file is assembled from records that already exist.

    The Invent practice

  50. Recover

    Find what the numbers are hiding.

  51. 43“The bank balance and the books haven’t matched in over a year.”Recover

    The solutionThe books rebuilt from bank and card statements, with each difference traced to the transaction that caused it and given a dollar figure.

    The Recover practice
  52. 44“We’re busy all year and the margin still isn’t there.”Recover

    The solutionA three-way match on purchases and a sweep for duplicate payments and unbilled retainage receivable, with findings ranked by dollar impact.

    The Recover practice
  53. 45“Our CPA mentioned the R&D credit once. Nobody followed up.”Recover

    The solutionA screen for the R&D credit and the Work Opportunity Tax Credit, with anything promising documented and handed to your CPA to claim.

    The Recover practice
  54. 46“A buyer wants three years of clean financials.”Recover

    The solutionA cleaned chart of accounts with consistent cost coding, and reconciled history documented well enough for your CPA or the buyer’s accountant to rely on.

    The Recover practice
  55. 47“The project has been waiting on a zoning variance since spring.”Recover

    The solutionEntitlements coordination that maps the requirements and who decides, keeping the process moving alongside your land-use attorney and engineer.

    The Recover practice
  56. 48“We’re probably eligible for HUBZone. We’ve never applied.”Recover

    The solutionCertification readiness for 8(a), HUBZone, WOSB, SDVOSB or DBE, with the eligibility file assembled before the application goes in.

    The Recover practice
