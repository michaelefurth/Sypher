Questions

Frequently asked

What makes Sypher Solutions a boutique firm?

Every engagement is led and delivered by the Managing Principal. There is no hand-off to a junior team. You get senior attention, a single point of accountability and a deliberately limited client roster, so your work never waits in a queue.

What is fractional leadership, and is it right for us?

Fractional leadership gives you an experienced operator, such as a COO, marketing lead or director of strategic operations, for a set number of days each month. It suits companies that need senior direction and execution but aren’t ready for a full-time executive hire.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes. We help founders test ideas, build business plans and feasibility studies, set up operations, launch a brand and website, and prepare for funding, grants and investor conversations.

Can a small business use AI without a large IT budget?

Absolutely. We start with the workflow, not the tool. We look for repetitive, high-volume work such as intake, scheduling, document preparation and reporting, then put practical AI and automation in place using the software you already pay for.

How are engagements priced?

Defined projects are scoped at a fixed fee. Fractional leadership and advisory run as monthly retainers. Every engagement is scoped in writing, with deliverables and milestones agreed before work begins.

Where do you work?

Sypher is based in the Southwest and works with clients nationwide. Engagements are remote-first, with on-site time where it matters.
