Questions

Questions, answered plainly.

What makes Sypher Solutions a boutique firm?

Every engagement is led by the Managing Principal, who stays your single point of accountability from the first call to the final handover. There is no hand-off to a junior team, and the client roster is deliberately small, so your work never waits in a queue. When a problem calls for a licensed specialist, they work under that same accountability.

Is any problem really “too big”?

We haven’t met one that couldn’t be broken down. Big problems are usually several smaller ones tangled together, and our method is built to separate them. Where a problem calls for a licensed specialist, such as a CPA, attorney or regulatory consultant, we bring them in and coordinate the work so you still have one point of accountability.

What is fractional leadership, and is it right for us?

Fractional leadership gives you an experienced operator, such as a COO, marketing lead or director of strategic operations, for a set number of days each month. It suits companies that need senior direction and execution but aren’t ready for a full-time executive hire.

What is forensic bookkeeping, and how is it different from an audit?

Forensic bookkeeping rebuilds and reconciles your records, traces transactions and surfaces discrepancies and leakage, such as unbilled work, duplicate payments or misclassified costs. It is not an audit or attest engagement. Sypher is not a CPA firm, and we organize our findings so your CPA or attorney can act on them.

Do you work on medical devices and formulations?

Yes, at the research and development planning stage. We help inventors and early teams define the problem, research prior art and literature, plan formulation and prototype work, source materials and lab partners, research likely regulatory pathways and pursue non-dilutive funding. Formal regulatory submissions are handled with qualified regulatory counsel.

Can a small business use AI without a large IT budget?

Absolutely. We start with the workflow, not the tool. We look for repetitive, high-volume work such as intake, scheduling, document preparation and reporting, then put practical AI and automation in place using the software you already pay for.

How are engagements priced?

Defined projects are scoped at a fixed fee. Fractional leadership and advisory run as monthly retainers. Every engagement is scoped in writing, with deliverables and milestones agreed before work begins.

Where do you work?

Sypher is based in the Southwest and works with clients nationwide. Engagements are remote-first, with on-site time where it matters.
