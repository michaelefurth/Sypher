The Firm & The Principal

A boutique firm, by design.

Sypher Solutions exists to give growing businesses something usually reserved for much larger clients: senior, cross-functional judgment, delivered personally and carried all the way through to results.

A Note from the Principal

“I started Sypher because I kept meeting capable owners who were stuck between advice they couldn’t implement and vendors who couldn’t see the whole business.”

My work has always lived where strategy meets execution. I’ve led strategic operations for a multi-state financial services firm, building its sales and marketing engine from the ground up. I’ve written feasibility studies for new ventures, navigated state workforce programs on behalf of employers, and designed the websites, content and SEO that help businesses get found.

My background also includes biomedical device and formulation research, where every result has to be earned from first principles, and forensic bookkeeping, where the answer is usually hiding in plain sight. That same discipline shapes how I approach every business problem.

Increasingly, I help clients put AI and automation to practical use: not as a novelty, but as a way to give small teams the capacity of much larger ones.

When you work with Sypher, you work with me directly. I keep my client roster small on purpose, so every engagement gets the attention, candor and follow-through it deserves.

The Name

Sypher takes its name from the cipher: a message that holds its meaning until someone takes the time to decode it.

Every business carries its own cipher: the tangle of priorities, systems and pressures that hides what to do next. Our work is to decode it patiently and rigorously, then reveal a solution that’s clear enough to act on. The ribbon in our mark is the same idea in visual form: many strands, one continuous path.
