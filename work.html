Selected Work

Complex problems, quietly solved.

Discretion is part of the service, so client names stay confidential. What follows is how we think and work: the challenge, the approach and what we delivered.

01

Financial Services · Multi-State

A complete sales operations system, built from the ground up.

  • Lead
  • Grow
  • Run

The challenge. A financial services firm serving construction and service contractors was expanding into new states. It needed a repeatable way to find the right prospects, start credible conversations and bring new sales hires up to speed quickly.

Our approach. Serving as fractional Director of Strategic Operations, we segmented the market by trade, metro, contract history and certification, building targeted prospect lists from public contract-award data. We then designed the operating system around it.

Delivered. A master sales playbook covering quick-start onboarding, a daily dashboard, scenario-based call scripts, objection handlers, a trade-specific pain-point matrix, follow-up templates, weekly scorecards, a seasonal campaign calendar and a referral engine. We also delivered CRM pipeline design, territory route strategy and a collateral series for contractors, subcontractors, primes, bankers, accountants and community referrers.

02

Construction Materials · Southwest

An investor-grade feasibility study for a new industrial venture.

  • Launch
  • Fund

The challenge. Founders exploring a construction-materials venture needed to know whether regional demand, competition and financing conditions justified the investment, and they needed to show lenders the same evidence.

Our approach. We built the case from public economic data, including population and permit trends and the regional pipeline of major construction projects. We then mapped the competitive landscape, including recent industry consolidation, and modelled operating and financing scenarios against current lending rules and the interest-rate environment.

Delivered. A decision-ready feasibility report with market analysis, competitive positioning, operating model and financing pathways, written for founders, partners and lenders alike.

03

Small Business · Workforce Development

Turning state workforce programs into a hiring strategy.

  • Fund
  • Run

The challenge. A growing company needed to add and train staff without straining cash flow, and didn’t know which public programs it qualified for.

Our approach. We worked directly with the regional workforce board to map On-the-Job Training reimbursements and Work-Based Learning programs to the company’s hiring plan, clarifying eligibility, documentation and monitoring requirements.

Delivered. A funding pathway for priority hires, with job descriptions, candidate documentation and the training-plan structure the programs require, connected to the owner’s growth plan.
