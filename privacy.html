Privacy

Last updated October 2026

Sypher Solutions respects your privacy and the confidentiality of your business. This notice explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

Information you share

When you contact us, by email or through the contact form, we receive the details you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, organization and message. We use this information only to respond to you and, if we work together, to deliver our services.

What we don’t do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We do not add you to marketing lists without your permission.
  • We do not name clients or share engagement details without explicit consent.

Website basics

This site does not use advertising trackers or third-party cookies, and its fonts and scripts are served from our own domain. If analytics are added in the future, this notice will be updated to describe them.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the information you have shared at any time by emailing michael@sypher.solutions.
