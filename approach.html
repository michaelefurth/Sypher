The Sypher Method

Every recommendation comes with a receipt.

Complexity is rarely the problem itself. Usually it’s what hides the problem. The method below exists to separate the two, so each recommendation traces back to something found in your business.

  1. Unravel

    01 · Discovery

    Unravel

    Interviews with the people who do the work and a read of the numbers and contracts. Then time spent watching a job move from sale to invoice.

    You receive: a plain written account of how the business runs today.

  2. Decode

    02 · Diagnosis

    Decode

    We separate symptoms from causes and find the one constraint that limits the rest. Options get weighed by payoff against effort.

    You receive: a short list, in priority order.

  3. Uncover

    03 · Design

    Uncover

    We write the roadmap: who owns each item, when it’s due, what it costs. Then the few metrics that will show whether it’s working.

    You receive: a plan your team can execute.

  4. Solve

    04 · Delivery

    Solve

    We build what the plan calls for and check it against the metrics. The documentation is handed over with it.

    You receive: systems that keep working after the engagement ends.

The Boutique Standard

What you can expect from us.

Principal-led by design. These are the commitments that come with it.

i.

Principal-led, always

The person you meet is the person who does the work. Nobody junior takes over once the contract is signed. When a job needs a licensed specialist, Michael brings one in and answers for the result.

ii.

Strategy that ships

Recommendations come with the implementation. A plan that sits in a shared drive hasn’t earned its fee.

iii.

Whole-business view

A marketing push can swamp operations; a grant can change the hiring plan. Fixing one thing shouldn’t break another.

iv.

Candor over comfort

You’ll hear what we see, plainly, including when the answer is “don’t do this yet” or “hire someone else.”

v.

Built to hand over

Accounts and files sit in your name from day one, and every process gets written down. You shouldn’t need us later.

vi.

Confidential by default

Your numbers and plans stay private. Clients are never named without written permission.

Ways to Engage

Three ways to work together.

Defined Project

Build

A fixed fee for one defined deliverable with a finish line: a feasibility study, a website, a grant application, a sales playbook for your team.

  • Scope and fee agreed in writing
  • Weekly check-ins on progress
  • Documented handover

Best when you know what needs to exist.

Fractional Leadership

Embed

A senior operator for a set number of days each month, sitting in your leadership meetings and owning agreed results. The goal is a team that can run without the retainer.

  • Monthly retainer with a set cadence
  • Owner of record for agreed KPIs
  • Team and vendor management

Best when the plan needs someone to run it.

Strategic Advisory

Advise

Scheduled sessions for an owner or founder who wants a second opinion before the big decisions, and someone to call between meetings.

  • Scheduled strategy sessions
  • Between-session access for key decisions
  • Quarterly priorities review

Best when you’re running it and want a second read.
