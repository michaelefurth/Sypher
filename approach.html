The Sypher Method

Rigor you can see. Results you can measure.

Complexity is rarely the real problem. It’s what hides the problem. Our method is designed to unravel it methodically, so every recommendation is grounded and every action has a reason.

  1. Unravel

    01 · Discovery

    Unravel

    Stakeholder conversations, a review of your numbers, systems, contracts and customer experience, and time spent seeing how work actually gets done.

    You receive: a candid current-state assessment.

  2. Decode

    02 · Diagnosis

    Decode

    We separate symptoms from causes, find the constraint that limits everything else, and weigh opportunities by impact, effort and risk.

    You receive: a prioritized short list, not a long one.

  3. Uncover

    03 · Design

    Uncover

    We reveal the path forward with a roadmap that names owners, milestones, budgets and the few metrics that will prove progress.

    You receive: a plan your team can execute.

  4. Solve

    04 · Delivery

    Solve

    We build: the systems, content, campaigns, applications and processes. Then we measure, refine and hand over clean documentation.

    You receive: working results that last after we leave.

The Boutique Standard

What you can expect from us.

A small firm is a deliberate choice. These are the commitments that come with it.

i.

Principal-led, always

The person you meet is the person who leads and does the work. No bait-and-switch and no junior bench. When specialists are needed, they work under that same accountability.

ii.

Strategy that ships

We don’t stop at recommendations. Plans are only valuable once they’re implemented, so implementation is part of the job.

iii.

Whole-business view

Marketing, operations, technology and funding are connected. We design across them, so fixing one doesn’t break another.

iv.

Candor over comfort

You’ll hear what we see, clearly and respectfully, including when the answer is “don’t do this yet.”

v.

Built to hand over

Everything is documented and owned by you: accounts, files, processes and know-how. Independence is the goal.

vi.

Confidential by default

Your numbers, plans and people stay private. We never name clients without explicit permission.

Ways to Engage

Three ways to work together.

Defined Project

Build

A clear scope, a fixed fee and a finished deliverable, such as a feasibility study, a website, a grant application or a new operating system for your team.

  • Scope, milestones and fee agreed upfront
  • Weekly progress check-ins
  • Documented handover

Best when you know what needs to exist.

Fractional Leadership

Embed

Senior operating leadership for a set number of days each month. We join your leadership rhythm, own outcomes and build the capability you’ll grow into.

  • Monthly retainer with a defined cadence
  • Ownership of agreed objectives and KPIs
  • Leadership meetings, team and vendor management

Best when you need a leader, not just a plan.

Strategic Advisory

Advise

A trusted sounding board for owners and founders: regular strategy sessions, decision support and on-call perspective when the stakes are high.

  • Scheduled strategy sessions
  • Between-session access for key decisions
  • Quarterly priorities review

Best when you lead, and want a partner in the room.
