The Practice

Eight practices. One person accountable.

The work is sorted by the situation you are in rather than by discipline. Most clients start with one practice; some need two or three joined into one plan, which is easier when one person holds all of it.

01

Launch

From idea to operating company.

Startup development for founders who would rather find the flaw on paper than in the bank account. The work centers on a monthly model with base and stress cases, carried far enough to show break-even volume and the working capital to reach it.

Ideal for first-time founders, and for established owners opening a new line of business.

Problems we solve in Launch

What’s included

  • Feasibility studiesUnit economics tested with sensitivity on price, ending in a verdict in plain words. Sometimes it is no.
  • Business plansA pro forma and use-of-funds table tied to the model, plus the narrative an underwriter reads.
  • Operations setupRegistrations and banking done in an order that avoids rework, before the first invoice.
  • Brand foundationsPositioning and a plain identity that look organized without pretending the company is old.
  • Go-to-marketOne segment to win first, priced to the margin you need, in a first-90-day plan.
  • Pitch materialsDecks and lender summaries built from the model, so story and spreadsheet agree.

Outcomes

  • A go / no-go decision with the gates written down
  • A capital stack sized to the stress case
  • A company that can bill a customer in its first weeks

02

Grow

Found, then followed up.

Marketing and sales built as one system, since traffic nobody follows up on is wasted. Search work covers Google Business Profile, NAP consistency and pages written to search intent. Sales work covers pipeline stages with exit criteria and a cadence people keep.

Ideal for businesses with a strong offer and an inconsistent pipeline.

Problems we solve in Grow

What’s included

  • Marketing strategyPositioning and an ideal customer profile first, then a channel plan sized to what you can spend.
  • SEO implementationFixes measured against Core Web Vitals, plus schema markup on the pages that sell.
  • Content & collateralWebsite copy and one-pagers for a buyer comparing you with two others.
  • Sales operationsScenario call scripts and objection handlers, plus territory and route planning.
  • CRM & pipelineStages with exit criteria and a weekly hygiene routine, so conversion means something.
  • Referral programsBankers and accountants as referral partners, with collateral for their clients.

Outcomes

  • Listings and pages that match what local buyers type
  • Stage conversion you can read off the CRM each week
  • A sales process a new hire can learn from the playbook

03

Run

Operations that don’t depend on you being in every room.

Project management and process design for owners who have become the bottleneck. The fix is usually unglamorous: decision rights written down, and a weekly scorecard reviewed in the same meeting every week.

Ideal for owner-led firms that have outgrown spreadsheets and memory.

Problems we solve in Run

What’s included

  • Project managementA critical path and risk register, with change orders priced before work starts.
  • Business administrationVendor terms reviewed and renewals on a compliance calendar, so nothing renews by surprise.
  • SOPs & playbooksProcedures written by watching the work, short enough that people use them.
  • KPI dashboardsA short list of numbers pulled weekly from systems you already use.
  • Operating rhythmA weekly scorecard meeting and a monthly review, each with a decision log.
  • Workforce planningRole definitions and onboarding plans that say what good looks like.

Outcomes

  • Projects with a named owner and a date on every task
  • Owners pulled in on exceptions only
  • A business a lender or buyer can follow without you there

04

Modernize

Technology that earns its keep.

IT and AI integration that begins with a workflow audit instead of a product demo. The gains usually sit in intake and document drafting, where the same information gets retyped. AI goes in with human review on anything a client will see, and MFA comes first.

Ideal for teams buried in manual work, or with a website that no longer represents them.

Problems we solve in Modernize

What’s included

  • AI integrationAssistants for intake and drafting, with data-handling guardrails and human review.
  • Workflow automationForms wired to the CRM and the books, so a record is entered once.
  • Web design & developmentAccessible sites that pass Core Web Vitals and that your staff can edit.
  • IT & systemsGoogle Workspace or Microsoft 365 with MFA and access control by role.
  • Data & reportingData cleaned at the source, feeding dashboards that answer Monday’s questions.
  • Team enablementShort written guides and a session, so the tools are still in use in six months.

Outcomes

  • Measured hours returned each week
  • A website that loads quickly and produces inquiries
  • Fewer subscriptions, each with a named owner

05

Fund

Grants screened before anyone writes.

Grant writing and funding strategy for businesses and mission-driven organizations. Each opportunity gets an eligibility screen against the NOFO and a check on cost share before a narrative is drafted, because plenty of programs that look like a fit fail one of the two.

Ideal for organizations with a fundable mission and no time to chase it.

Problems we solve in Fund

What’s included

  • Funding researchA ranked list of programs screened for eligibility, with reimbursement terms noted.
  • Grant readinessSAM.gov and UEI started early, and budgets with indirect costs set as the funder allows.
  • Grant writingNarratives built on a logic model, with letters of commitment collected early enough to count.
  • Workforce programsOn-the-Job Training and Work-Based Learning, mapped to your hiring plan.
  • Proposals & RFPsCapability statements and a compliance matrix for each solicitation.
  • Post-award supportA reporting calendar, with expenses kept on the award’s reimbursement basis.

Outcomes

  • A short list of programs you qualify for, with deadlines
  • Applications written to the scoring criteria
  • Records ready for the first post-award report

06

Lead

Senior leadership, bought by the month.

Fractional and interim leadership: an experienced operator in your business for the days each month that matter. Each objective gets KPIs and an owner of record, and the handover plan is written in the first month, since the job is to become unnecessary.

Ideal for companies that need executive capacity before they need an executive hire.

Problems we solve in Lead

What’s included

  • Fractional COOThe operating cadence and scorecard, until staff can own them.
  • Fractional CMOMarketing budget and agencies, with spend tied to pipeline.
  • Strategic operationsDirector-level ownership of a revenue initiative, like the sales system on the Work page.
  • Interim leadershipCover for a leadership gap, ending in a handover the new hire can use.
  • Strategic advisoryA sounding board for owners, prepared from your numbers.
  • Hiring supportA hiring plan for the permanent role, written once the function has shown what it needs.

Outcomes

  • Senior judgment at a fraction of full-time cost
  • An owner of record for the stalled initiative
  • A role defined well enough to hire into later

07

Invent

Biomedical & formulation R&D, from first principles.

Research and development planning for inventors and early teams working on medical devices and formulations. The expensive mistakes happen early, when user needs never get written down or prior art turns up after the prototype. The plan puts a decision gate at each step from concept to bench work to the evidence a partner will want.

Ideal for inventors and clinicians, and for early life-science teams.

Problems we solve in Invent

What’s included

  • Problem & needs definitionUser needs and intended use written down first, because they become the design inputs everything is tested against.
  • Literature & prior artA structured search of the published literature and patent prior art, done before the concept hardens.
  • Device concept developmentConcept options compared on their trade-offs, with a staged prototyping plan and decision gates.
  • Formulation research planningExcipient review and a design of experiments, with stability testing planned before the first batch.
  • Sourcing & lab partnersContract labs and CDMOs shortlisted against a written specification, with quotes compared side by side.
  • Regulatory pathway researchClassification and predicate device research on 510(k) or De Novo, organized for your regulatory counsel.
  • Research documentationDesign controls habits from day one, so the design history file is compiled later instead of reconstructed.
  • Non-dilutive fundingSBIR Phase I and II strategy timed to your development milestones.

Outcomes

  • A concept tested against written user needs
  • A staged R&D plan with a budget at each decision gate
  • Records a partner or investor can run diligence on

Sypher plans and coordinates R&D. Regulatory submissions are made with qualified regulatory counsel, and clinical work and testing by qualified clinical and laboratory partners.

08

Recover

Find what the numbers are hiding.

Forensic bookkeeping and entitlements work for owners who suspect money is leaking or going unclaimed. The books are rebuilt from bank and card statements and every account is tied out. Each exception is followed to its source and given a dollar figure. The second half covers what you may be entitled to and have never claimed.

Ideal for owners heading into a sale or a loan review, or who sense the numbers don’t add up.

Problems we solve in Recover

What’s included

  • Forensic bookkeepingBank reconciliation for every account, with each difference traced to the transaction behind it.
  • Leakage reviewsA three-way match of PO, receiving ticket and invoice, plus a sweep for duplicate payments.
  • Financial reconstructionA cleaned chart of accounts and cost coding your CPA can follow.
  • Credits & incentives reviewA screen for the R&D credit and Work Opportunity Tax Credit, passed to your CPA.
  • CertificationsReadiness files for 8(a), HUBZone, WOSB, SDVOSB or DBE, built to each program’s rules.
  • Land-use & permit entitlementsZoning and variance requirements mapped, with the process coordinated alongside your land-use attorney and engineer.

Outcomes

  • Books that reconcile, with a written account of what went wrong
  • Recovered cash, and controls that stop the same leak recurring
  • Credits and certifications you qualify for, filed instead of noted

Sypher is not a CPA or law firm. It does not issue audit or attest reports or give tax or legal opinions. Findings are prepared for your CPA or attorney to review and act on.
