The Practice

Six practices. One accountable partner.

Our services are grouped around the moments businesses actually face: launching, growing, running, modernizing, funding and leading. Engage one, or let us connect several into one coherent plan.

01

Launch

From idea to operating company.

Startup development for founders who want to build with discipline. We pressure-test the idea, quantify the opportunity and stand up the structure, systems and story you need to open for business and earn trust from customers, lenders and investors.

Ideal for founders, new ventures and established owners launching a new line of business.

What’s included

  • Feasibility studiesMarket sizing, demand drivers, competition and unit economics, assembled into a decision-ready report.
  • Business plansLender- and investor-ready plans with financial projections that hold up to scrutiny.
  • Operations setupEntity and registrations checklist, tools, vendors, banking and back-office foundations.
  • Brand foundationsName, positioning, identity and messaging that make a young company look established.
  • Go-to-marketIdeal customers, offers, pricing logic and first-90-day launch plan.
  • Pitch materialsDecks, one-pagers and capability statements for investors, lenders and partners.

Outcomes

  • A clear go / no-go decision, made on evidence
  • A business that is set up correctly from day one
  • Materials that open conversations with capital and customers

02

Grow

Be found. Be chosen.

Marketing and sales built as one system. We sharpen your positioning, implement technical and local SEO so the right buyers find you, and build the sales operations (scripts, pipeline, CRM and reporting) that turn attention into revenue.

Ideal for businesses with a strong offer and an inconsistent pipeline.

What’s included

  • Marketing strategyPositioning, audience segments, messaging and a channel plan sized to your budget.
  • SEO implementationTechnical audits, on-page optimization, structured data, local SEO and Google Business Profile.
  • Content & collateralWebsite copy, brochures, partner briefs, case studies and sales one-pagers.
  • Sales operationsPlaybooks, call scripts, objection handling, territory and route planning.
  • CRM & pipelineCRM selection and setup, pipeline stages, follow-up cadences and hygiene.
  • Referral programsStructured partner channels with bankers, accountants and allied firms.

Outcomes

  • Higher-quality organic traffic and local visibility
  • A repeatable, measurable sales process your team can run
  • A brand presence that matches the quality of your work

03

Run

Operations that don’t depend on you being in every room.

Project management, administration and process design for owners who have become the bottleneck. We bring structure to how work is planned, assigned, tracked and reported, then document it so the business runs consistently.

Ideal for owner-led firms that have outgrown spreadsheets and memory.

What’s included

  • Project managementScoping, schedules, budgets, risk logs and delivery management for critical initiatives.
  • Business administrationBack-office organization, vendor management, documentation and compliance calendars.
  • SOPs & playbooksClear, usable standard operating procedures and onboarding guides.
  • KPI dashboardsThe handful of numbers that matter, reported weekly, without manual effort.
  • Operating rhythmMeeting cadences, scorecards and accountability structures for leadership teams.
  • Workforce planningRoles, job descriptions, training plans and hiring workflows.

Outcomes

  • Projects delivered on time, on budget and visible to everyone
  • Owners freed from day-to-day firefighting
  • A business that is easier to scale, finance or sell

04

Modernize

Technology that earns its keep.

Practical IT and AI integration for businesses that want results, not experiments. We start with the workflow, find the hours being lost to repetitive work, and then implement AI, automation and modern web experiences using tools you can own and maintain.

Ideal for teams buried in manual work, or with a website that no longer represents them.

What’s included

  • AI integrationAssistants for intake, document drafting, research and reporting, with clear guardrails.
  • Workflow automationConnected forms, CRMs, spreadsheets and email, without manual re-keying.
  • Web design & developmentFast, elegant, accessible websites engineered for search and conversion.
  • IT & systemsSoftware selection, Google Workspace / Microsoft 365 setup, security basics.
  • Data & reportingClean data structures and dashboards that answer real business questions.
  • Team enablementTraining and documentation so your people adopt the tools with confidence.

Outcomes

  • Hours returned to your team every week
  • A website that works as hard as you do
  • Technology decisions made with a clear return in mind

05

Fund

Capital and programs, pursued with rigor.

Grant writing and funding strategy for businesses and mission-driven organizations. We identify the programs that fit, get you ready to compete, write compelling and compliant applications, and help you manage the reporting that follows an award.

Ideal for organizations with a fundable mission and no time to chase it.

What’s included

  • Funding researchA prioritized pipeline of grants, incentives and programs matched to your goals.
  • Grant readinessRegistrations, budgets, narratives, letters of support and organizational documents.
  • Grant writingPersuasive, compliant applications for federal, state, local and foundation funders.
  • Workforce programsNavigation of on-the-job training and work-based learning reimbursement programs.
  • Proposals & RFPsCapability statements and responses for public and private procurement.
  • Post-award supportReporting calendars, outcome tracking and compliance documentation.

Outcomes

  • A focused funding pipeline instead of scattered searching
  • Stronger, more competitive applications
  • Confidence managing awards once they arrive

06

Lead

Senior leadership, at the scale you need.

Fractional and interim leadership that brings an experienced operator into your business for the days each month that matter most. We join your leadership rhythm, own clear objectives and build the systems and team that will eventually carry them without us.

Ideal for companies that need executive capacity before they need an executive hire.

What’s included

  • Fractional COOOperations, people, process and performance management.
  • Fractional CMOMarketing leadership, budgets, agencies and growth strategy.
  • Strategic operationsDirector-level ownership of cross-functional initiatives and revenue operations.
  • Interim leadershipSteady hands during transitions, launches or leadership gaps.
  • Strategic advisoryA trusted sounding board for owners and boards.
  • Hiring supportDefining, recruiting and onboarding your permanent leader when you’re ready.

Outcomes

  • Senior judgment at a fraction of a full-time cost
  • Momentum on the initiatives that keep slipping
  • A leadership role you can confidently hire into later
